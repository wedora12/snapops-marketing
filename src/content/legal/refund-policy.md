## 1. Subscription Billing
SnapOps AI subscriptions are billed monthly or annually, depending on the plan you select, in advance via Razorpay. By subscribing, you authorize us to charge your chosen payment method on a recurring basis until you cancel.

## 2. Free Trial
SnapOps offers a free trial period of 15 days. No payment method is required to start your trial, and you will not be charged unless you actively choose to subscribe to a paid plan before or after your trial ends. If you do not subscribe, your trial simply expires and no charge occurs.

## 3. Cancellation
- You may cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings, or by emailing [SUPPORT EMAIL].
- Cancellation stops future billing but does not entitle you to a refund for the current billing period already paid.
- Upon cancellation, you retain access to the Service until the end of your current paid billing period.

## 4. Refund Eligibility
All fees are non-refundable except as required by applicable law. Because we offer a free trial (see Section 2) that lets you evaluate the Service fully before any payment is taken, we do not provide refunds or credits once a paid subscription payment has been made — including for partial billing periods, unused features, downgrades, or early cancellation.
