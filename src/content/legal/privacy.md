# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: [DATE]**

This Privacy Policy explains how Nuptial Diaries, a proprietorship registered in India ("SnapOps," "we," "us," "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use SnapOps AI (the "Service").

This policy is written with reference to India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and general good practice — it must be reviewed by a lawyer familiar with current DPDP Act rules and any applicable international regulations (e.g. GDPR, if you have or expect users/clients based in the EU/UK) before publication.

## 1. Information We Collect

**Account information:** name, email address, phone number, business name, and billing details when you sign up.

**Customer Data you upload:** information about your clients that you input into SnapOps, which may include names, contact details, wedding dates, payment records, and photographs. This is uploaded by you, the studio owner ("Data Fiduciary" under the DPDP Act), and we process it on your behalf ("Data Processor").

**Usage data:** how you interact with the Service — pages visited, features used, log data, device/browser information.

**Payment information:** processed via Razorpay; we do not directly store full payment card details.

## 2. How We Use Information
- To provide, operate, and maintain the Service.
- To process payments and manage subscriptions.
- To communicate with you about your account, updates, or support requests.
- To improve the Service, including through aggregated, anonymized usage analysis.
- To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information or your Customer Data to third parties.

## 9. Cookies and Tracking
We do not use analytics tools, tracking cookies, or similar technologies beyond what's strictly necessary for the Service to function (e.g., session/authentication cookies required to keep you logged in). We do not track your usage patterns for marketing or analytics purposes.

## 10. Children's Privacy
The Service is not directed at individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.
